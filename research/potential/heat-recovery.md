# Heat Recovery Evidence

## Idea

Prove useful heat recovery from data centers.

## Problem

Data centers produce heat that is often wasted.

## How It Works

1. Electricity consumption recorded.
2. Heat output measured.
3. Heat delivered to district network recorded.
4. Avoided cooling energy calculated.
5. Full energy lifecycle proven.

## Technical Dependencies

- Heat meters
- Temperature sensors
- Measurement Boundary
- Evidence Graph

## Why Later

Requires heat metering and district heating integration.

Strong ESG story after core energy evidence works.
