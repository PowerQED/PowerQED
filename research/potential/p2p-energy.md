# P2P Energy Trading

## Idea

Direct energy trading between producers and consumers.

## Problem

Small producers cannot sell excess energy directly.

## How It Works

1. Producer meter records generation.
2. Consumer meter records consumption.
3. Evidence layer verifies both.
4. Smart contract matches supply and demand.
5. Settlement uses verified interval data.

## Technical Dependencies

- Measurement Boundary
- Signed meter events
- Energy Evidence Protocol
- Smart contract settlement

## Why Later

Requires regulatory clarity and grid operator integration.

Evidence layer must prove generation and consumption first.
