# Grid Flexibility Settlement

## Idea

Verify demand response and flexibility events.

## Problem

Grid operators cannot easily verify claimed response.

## How It Works

1. Baseline established from historical evidence.
2. Dispatch event recorded.
3. Actual telemetry signed.
4. Response calculated by policy engine.
5. Proof issued for settlement.

## Technical Dependencies

- Baseline methodology
- Signed meter events
- Policy engine
- Optional ZK for selective disclosure

## Why Later

Requires VPP and grid operator partnerships.

MVP focuses on one site first.
