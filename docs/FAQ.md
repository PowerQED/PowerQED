# PowerQED Overview

## What PowerQED Is

PowerQED is a cryptographic evidence layer for physical energy.

It converts metered energy, device identity and operational events into independently verifiable, policy-bound claims.

## Blockchain Role

Blockchain anchors commitments and settlement.

PowerQED owns evidence, policy and claims.

## Evidence Infrastructure for 24/7 CFE

PowerQED is the evidence infrastructure on which 24/7 carbon-free energy claims are built.

## What PowerQED Proves

PowerQED proves how, when and under which rules electricity was generated, stored, consumed and matched with carbon-free energy.

## Measurement Boundary

A signed statement of all energy sources and loads at a site.

Without a complete boundary, signed data can be a lie through omission.

## Evidence Assurance

Every source is evaluated across identity, authentication, integrity, attestation, metrology, time, provenance, completeness and market eligibility.

## First Customers

AI data center developers, colocation providers, GPU cloud providers, energy retailers and battery operators.

## Business Model

Site fee plus per metered MWh plus per settlement claim.

## Infrastructure Independence

Eigen is the current infrastructure choice.

DA, compute, proof engine and attestation are abstractions.

## MVP

One data center campus with revenue-grade meters, one renewable source, one battery, one backup generator, signed events, measurement boundary, policy engine and verifiable receipt.
