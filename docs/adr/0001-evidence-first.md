# ADR 0001: Evidence First, Not Blockchain First

## Status

Accepted

## Context

PowerQED starts with physical energy evidence for AI data centers.

There is pressure to present blockchain, ZK and Eigen stack as the core.

## Decision

The core is Energy Evidence Protocol, Evidence Assurance Model and Evidence Graph.

Blockchain, ZK and Eigen are backend implementations behind interfaces.

## Consequences

- PowerQED survives changes in crypto infrastructure.
- First MVP can ship without custom ZK circuits.
- Investors see a real product layer, not a crypto experiment.
- Eigen and Ethereum remain as current infrastructure choices.

## Alternatives Considered

Blockchain-first architecture: rejected. Technology would lead the problem.
