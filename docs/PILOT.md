# Pilot Plan

## Goal

Prove that PowerQED produces trustworthy energy evidence for one real site.

## Scope

One data center campus.

One revenue-grade facility meter.

One solar or wind source.

One BESS.

One backup generator.

15-minute or 1-hour interval.

## Data Sources

- Grid meter
- Solar or wind meter
- BESS meter
- Backup generator meter
- Data center load meter

## Claims to Prove

24/7 Carbon-Free Energy Matching.

Verified Flexibility baseline and response.

## Success Criteria

Real site produces signed events.

Measurement Boundary complete.

Policy engine evaluates CFE claim.

Verification Receipt independently verifiable.

Partner confirms evidence is useful for audit or settlement.

## Deliverables

Signed event stream.

Measurement Boundary.

Evidence Graph for the pilot period.

Verification Receipt.

Pilot report.

## Next Step

Find pilot partner: AI data center developer, colocation provider, or energy retailer.
