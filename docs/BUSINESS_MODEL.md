# Business Model

## Revenue Model

PowerQED monetizes energy evidence, not users.

- Platform fee per site
- Fee per metered MWh
- Fee per settlement claim
- Enterprise API access

## Why This Works

The value of PowerQED grows with the volume of energy it verifies.

Not with the number of UI users.

## Customers

AI data center developers.

Colocation providers.

GPU cloud providers.

Energy retailers selling 24/7 CFE.

Battery and VPP operators.

## Examples

A data center campus with 10 GWh monthly consumption.

Platform fee plus per MWh fee.

Each 24/7 CFE claim generates additional value.

## Long-Term

Verified flexibility settlement.

Granular certificate integration.

EnergyTag compatibility.

Marketplace for verified energy claims.

## Principle

Evidence infrastructure scales with energy, not with users.
