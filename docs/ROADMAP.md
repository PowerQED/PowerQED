## Phase 1: Pilot MVP

3–4 months. $170–200K.

One data center campus.

Revenue-grade facility meter.

One solar or wind source.

One BESS.

One backup generator.

Signed events, Measurement Boundary, Merkle batches, policy engine, Evidence Graph, verifiable receipt.

Full EV fleet integration.

Marketplace for verified energy claims.

Grid flexibility settlement.
